# SPEC — rileytrottier.com

## What it is
Riley Trottier's personal site and blog. It shows his work as a Senior Product Manager building with AI: his projects, his AI portfolio, and articles about what he's built and learned.

## Who it's for
Recruiters, product managers and other people who search for Riley and want to see his work. The site exists for credibility.

## Goals
- Be one of the top results when someone searches "Riley Trottier".
- Make his projects and writing easy to find and quick to read.
- Stay consistent with his LinkedIn profile and GitHub (same story, not identical text).

## What's on it
- **Front page:** newspaper-style ("dispatches") layout; the newest post is the lead story.
- **Blog posts:** Markdown in `posts/`. Each post has at least one image (screenshots or his own diagrams).
- **Pages:** About, Work, Projects, Colophon (in `pages/`).
- **Themes:** light, dark and an optional motion mode.

## Must stay true
- Static site: Markdown in, plain HTML out. No database, no user accounts.
- Fast and readable on phone and desktop.
- Fonts: Spectral and Schibsted Grotesk.
- His title reads "Senior Product Manager" everywhere.
- Nothing private, strategic or financial about his employer goes on the site.
- Nothing is published without Riley checking it first.

## Not building
- Comments, logins or newsletters on the site.
- A CMS or admin panel; posts are files in the repo.
- Tracking beyond privacy-friendly visitor counts.
